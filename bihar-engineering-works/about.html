Welcome to
Bihar Engineering Works

Established in 1984, Bihar Engineering Works (BEW) has earned a trusted reputation as a leading manufacturer and solution provider in the Refractory, Crushing, Grinding, and Mixing industries. With over four decades of experience, BEW specializes in designing and manufacturing high-performance Electric Servo Press machines and accessories.

Backed by a skilled team of engineers and industry experts, we deliver end-to-end solutions — from concept and design to commissioning and service support. At BEW, innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction remain at the core of everything we do.
